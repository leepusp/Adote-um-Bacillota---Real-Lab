BLAST: one best 16S hit per accession

Reduce thousands of aligned BLAST regions to the highest-bit-score alignment for each genomic accession. Choose the two files downloaded from NCBI BLAST. No terminal, installation, or internet required.

BLAST results → Download → Hit Table (CSV). Requires the standard 12 BLAST fields, including subject accession, subject coordinates and bit score.

BLAST results → Download → FASTA (aligned sequences). Headers should begin with an accession and coordinates, e.g. NZ_CP040336.1:486692-487421.

Select your two files, then run the filter.
